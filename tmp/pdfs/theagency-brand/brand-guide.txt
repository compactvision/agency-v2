DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
The Design Philosophy is a series of beliefs that identify the benefits our brand offers the consumer. These benefits can be expressed on three levels: physical,
rational and emotional. These benefits aim to capture our design approach.
Simply put:
PHYSICAL Benefits
• How is the brand designed? I applaud innovation through its creative insight.
• Does the brand fit the purpose? I look beyond the obvious and find something simply unique.
RATIONALLY Benefits
• Why should I choose to use the brand? It’s the real.
• What is the reasons to buy the brand? I feel assured that there is no substitute for this value.
EMOTIONALLY Benefits
• How does the brand make me feel? My social concerns are enhanced by my brand’s responsibility and conscience.
• Does it give a relationship reward greater than the cost of the transaction Yes, and more.

INTERACTION DESIGN appreciates service design to enliven culture changes. Our interaction strategy looks at the activities and processes that need to be
transformed to provide better services for consumers.
When designing interactions, we focus on five principles to activate optimal service experience:
• User-centered by understanding the user through qualitative research;
• Co-creative by involving all relevant stakeholders including distribution partners in the design process;
• Sequencing by partitioning a complex service into separate processes;
• Evidencing by visualizing service experiences and making them tangible; and
• Holistic by considering touch points in a network of interactions and users.

and even outside apps and websites. Businesses use social advertising campaigns to build brand awareness, generate leads, and/or capture sales revenue. Social
advertising is a relatively low-cost approach that offers ultra-specific user data and unmatched targeting abilities. During the course of the campaigns, we can easily LINKEDIN FOR BUSINESS
assess and optimize ad performance and count on steadier, more predictable response rates.
LinkedIn is the world’s leading career- and professional networking-oriented site. The platform currently boasts 610 million users, with 4 out of 5 in decision-making
Social advertising campaign has three pillars: a well-defined strategy, detailed planning, and streamlined execution roles. Cost per click can run higher than on other platforms, but for companies looking to target business to business prospects, LinkedIn is a solid advertising
investment.

OBJECTIVES
• Define and communicate the Brand Vision;
• Encourage each employee to be a Brand Champion within the business;
• Align functional business goals, objectives and initiatives with Brand Values;
• Develop a culture of cross functional insight and input on Brand decisions;
• Influence Brand equity developments.
SCOPE
• Enliven brand values throughout the organization;
• By defining, communicating and institutionalizing consumer interaction platforms throughout the business;
• Set of core Brand Values that will result in a cohesive culture and a common frame of reference for all employees. Leadership should:
- Visibly express their commitment towards the Core Values
- Understand their individual and team roles and be responsible for literally living out the Core Values (i.e. set the example)
• Employees (including leadership) should:
- Be aware of the Core Values;
- Understand the relevance of the Core Values;
- Be able to determine behavior that constitutes the Core Values; and
- Accept the behavioral changes required to `live out` the Core Values.
